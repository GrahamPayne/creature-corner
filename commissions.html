Commissions

Custom creature sculptures, masks, and sci-fi artifacts, designed and built by hand.

I take on a limited number of custom commissions, ranging from original creature sculptures and masks to painted artifacts and one-of-a-kind collectibles. Every piece is designed and built by hand, so availability is limited and not every inquiry can be accepted.

The Process

  1. 1

    Inquiry

    Submit the form below with a description of what you'd like made, any reference images or links, and your rough budget and timeline. Submitting an inquiry does not guarantee acceptance.

  2. 2

    Review

    I'll review your inquiry to make sure the project is a good fit for my style, schedule, and current commission queue, and will follow up by email.

  3. 3

    Quote, deposit, and approval

    If I can take on the project, I will provide a written quote and estimated schedule. A 50% non-refundable deposit is required before work begins.

  4. 4

    Work in progress

    Once the deposit is received, work begins. I'll share progress updates as the piece moves through sculpting, painting, and finishing.

  5. 5

    Completion and delivery

    The remaining 50% is due when the artwork is finished. Shipping, delivery, or pickup will be arranged after the final payment has been received.

Payment Terms

Accepted commissions require a 50% non-refundable deposit before work begins. The remaining 50% is due when the artwork is finished and must be paid before shipping, delivery, or pickup.
