# Field Guide — Lore Rules (binding for all future work)

The Field Guide is a multidimensional device created to record and preserve
anomalies. It is the continuing central character of Creature Corner. It can
connect Graham Payne's creatures, artifacts, paintings, sculptures,
environments, memories, specimens, and other original artwork.

## Never establish

- Whether it is alive, conscious, evil, possessed, hungry, or hunting anyone
- That it is a normal artificial intelligence
- Its creator, original civilization, or planet of origin
- A religion around it, or a magical origin
- Fixed cartridge classes or fixed storage limits
- An explanation for every organic reaction
- A final visitor storyline or a final ending

It may *appear* to act independently. Why is never defined.

## Voice

Mysterious, curious, technological, organic, ancient, slightly unsettling,
playful when appropriate — familiar in some places, impossible in others.
Not every response is threatening or dramatic. Responses are short, uppercase,
and non-committal about the device's nature (see `src/features/field-guide/data/responses.js`;
all copy there is prototype writing, freely editable).

## May eventually appear (none required)

Familiar electronics, stone, minerals, crystals, organic growth, yellow
slime-mold-like growth, living reactive windows, temporal irregularities.

## Placeholder data

Prototype records (`TEST-CR-*`, `TEST-AR-*`, `TEST-ME-*`) are clearly marked
and are **not** official lore. Do not invent permanent creatures, artifacts,
histories, dimensions, or scientific names in code or data files.

## Artwork ownership

All Creature Corner artwork — creatures, sculptures, models, paintings,
artifacts, and visual development, including the Field Guide device mockup —
is original work by Graham Payne. It is never AI-generated, never created by
Claude or by this application, and never sourced from asset libraries.
Claude assists with the software only.
